Treasure Arrows

Privacy Policy

Developer: CodeS Dev Lab
Effective date: November 17, 2026

CodeS Dev Lab respects your privacy. Treasure Arrows is a casual puzzle game in which players solve arrow challenges on treasure maps, explore adventure-themed locations, and discover virtual treasures. The app is designed to operate without collecting, storing, uploading, or sharing users' personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Treasure Arrows does not collect personal or sensitive user information. We do not ask users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal details to play.

The app does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, profiling, or marketing.

2. Device Permissions

The app is designed not to request Android system permissions to access personal or sensitive device information. It does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, or other personal files for gameplay.

3. Gameplay and Local Data

The game involves tapping arrows to clear puzzles on illustrated treasure maps and revealing virtual treasure rewards. If gameplay progress or preferences are stored locally on your device, they are used only to support the game experience. Such local game information is not uploaded to or collected by CodeS Dev Lab.

4. How We Use or Share Data

Because the app does not collect personal data, CodeS Dev Lab does not use, sell, rent, or share personal data collected through the app with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

CodeS Dev Lab does not maintain accounts or a server-side database of users' personal information for this app. Therefore, we do not retain personal data collected through the app, and there is no app account to delete.

If local game data exists on your device, you can usually remove it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

6. Data Security

We follow a data-minimization approach: the app is designed so that personal information does not need to be collected or transmitted for normal gameplay. Local app data, if any, is subject to the security controls of your device and Android operating system.

7. Children's Privacy

Treasure Arrows contains general-audience, family-friendly puzzle content, but it is not specifically designed for or directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users through the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change, or to comply with applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. If a future version introduces data collection or third-party services that process user information, this policy will be updated to describe those practices.
